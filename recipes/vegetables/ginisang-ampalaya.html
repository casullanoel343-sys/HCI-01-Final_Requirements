Ginisang Ampalaya Recipe

by Panlasang Pinoy

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. 1

    Slice the ampalaya thinly and soak it in salted water for 10 minutes to reduce bitterness.

  2. 2

    Drain and gently squeeze the ampalaya. Set aside.

  3. 3

    Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.

  4. 4

    Sauté garlic, onion, and tomatoes until softened and fragrant.

  5. 5

    Add the ampalaya and stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes.

  6. 6

    Add oyster sauce, salt, and pepper. Mix well.

  7. 7

    Pour the beaten eggs into the pan and allow them to cook slightly before stirring.

  8. 8

    Continue cooking until the eggs are fully cooked. Serve hot with steamed rice.
