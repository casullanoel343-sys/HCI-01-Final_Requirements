Ginisang Monggo Recipe

by Panlasang Pinoy

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. 1

    Wash the monggo thoroughly and place it in a pot with water.

  2. 2

    Boil the monggo for 30 to 40 minutes or until soft. Set aside.

  3. 3

    Heat cooking oil in a separate pan over medium heat.

  4. 4

    Sauté garlic, onion, and tomatoes until soft and fragrant.

  5. 5

    Add the sliced pork belly and cook until lightly browned.

  6. 6

    Pour the sautéed mixture into the cooked monggo.

  7. 7

    Add fish sauce, salt, and pepper. Stir well and simmer for 5 minutes.

  8. 8

    Add spinach or malunggay leaves and cook for 1 to 2 minutes.

  9. 9

    Transfer to a serving bowl and serve hot with steamed rice.
