Pinakbet Recipe

by Panlasang Pinoy

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. 1

    Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Sauté garlic, onion, and tomatoes until fragrant and softened.

  3. 3

    Add the sliced pork belly and cook until lightly browned.

  4. 4

    Add the bagoong alamang and stir well.

  5. 5

    Pour in water and bring to a gentle boil.

  6. 6

    Add the squash first and cook for 3 minutes.

  7. 7

    Add eggplant, sitaw, and ampalaya. Stir gently.

  8. 8

    Cover the pan and simmer until the vegetables are tender.

  9. 9

    Season with salt and pepper according to taste.

  10. 10

    Transfer to a serving bowl and serve hot with steamed rice.
