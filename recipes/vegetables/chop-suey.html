Chopsuey Recipe

by Panlasang Pinoy

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. 1

    Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Sauté garlic and onion until fragrant and translucent.

  3. 3

    Add the chicken strips and cook until lightly browned.

  4. 4

    Add the shrimp and cook until pink.

  5. 5

    Pour in soy sauce and oyster sauce. Stir well to coat the meat and vegetables.

  6. 6

    Add cauliflower, carrots, and snow peas. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes.

  7. 7

    Pour in the chicken broth and let it simmer briefly.

  8. 8

    Add cabbage and bell pepper. Stir gently until vegetables are tender-crisp.

  9. 9

    Add the dissolved cornstarch and stir until the sauce thickens slightly.

  10. 10

    Transfer to a serving plate and serve hot with steamed rice.
