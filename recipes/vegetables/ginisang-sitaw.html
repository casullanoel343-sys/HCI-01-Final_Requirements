Ginisang Sitaw Recipe

by Panlasang Pinoy

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. 1

    Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.

  2. 2

    Sauté garlic, onion, and tomatoes until fragrant and softened.

  3. 3

    Add the ground pork and cook until lightly browned.

  4. 4

    Pour in the soy sauce and stir well.

  5. 5

    Add the sitaw and mix thoroughly with the meat and seasonings.

  6. 6

    Pour in water and allow it to simmer for 5 to 7 minutes until the sitaw becomes tender.

  7. 7

    Season with salt and pepper according to taste.

  8. 8

    Transfer to a serving plate and serve hot with steamed rice.
